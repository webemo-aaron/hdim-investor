# Production Readiness

## Summary

The current public-safe readiness posture for HDIM is: technically substantial, supported by real readiness artifacts, but best represented with explicit disclosure of documentation-governance work rather than with absolute or promotional certainty.

## What Supports The Readiness Story

- code-validated architecture inventory
- generated service and dependency catalogs
- shared validation infrastructure
- consumer/provider contract testing in at least one domain
- internal readiness and review artifacts available for deeper diligence

## Current Public Read

From a public-safe standpoint, the right message is:

- the platform is materially built
- the platform has real cross-cutting controls
- the platform has meaningful engineering and validation evidence
- some governance cleanup is still underway and is being documented transparently

## Current Disclosed Gaps

Public materials should acknowledge:

- contract publication is not yet uniformly complete across the platform
- frontend endpoint ownership still needs further normalization
- shared contract modules are not yet uniformly materialized as published artifacts

## Why This Is Still A Positive Read

The platform’s credibility comes from disclosing and addressing these gaps instead of pretending they do not exist. That is stronger diligence behavior than overstating platform coherence.

## What Moves To NDA

Under NDA, the readiness story can be expanded with:

- technical review packets
- investor readiness reviews
- validation reports
- deeper architecture and contract-governance findings
